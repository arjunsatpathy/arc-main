Appointment of Chairperson and Member

The Government of Arunachal Pradesh has appointed Shri Pramod Kumar Jain, IAS (Retd.) as the Chairperson of the Arunachal Pradesh Administrative Reforms Commission and Shri Hento Karga, APCS (Retd.) as Member of the Commission. The Commission has been entrusted with the responsibility of examining the existing administrative system of the State and recommending suitable reforms to improve governance, administrative efficiency, transparency, accountability, and public service delivery. The constitution of the Commission reflects the commitment of the State Government towards strengthening governance and making the administration more responsive and citizen-centric.
