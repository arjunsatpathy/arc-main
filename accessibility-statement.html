Accessibility Statement

Arunachal Pradesh Administrative Reforms Commission

Home / Policies & Help / Accessibility

We are fully committed to ensuring that the official website of the Arunachal Pradesh Administrative Reforms Commission (APARC) is accessible to all users irrespective of device in use, assistive technology, or physical ability. It has been built following the Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW 3.0) and World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards.

Accessibility Features

  • Text Size Adjustments: Built-in controls at the top of every page allow users to decrease (A-), reset to normal (A), or increase (A+) text size without disrupting layout flow.
  • Skip to Main Content: Keyboard users can bypass repetitive navigation links directly to the primary page content via the skip link.
  • Screen Reader Compatibility: Dynamic notifications, table headers with scope attributes, and clear ARIA landmark roles ensure screen readers interpret content accurately.
  • Keyboard Navigability: All interactive elements, dropdown menus, tabs, accordions, and dialog modals can be navigated using Tab, Enter, and Arrow keys with clearly visible focus rings.
  • Color Contrast: Text and essential interactive components maintain high color contrast ratios against backgrounds for effortless legibility.

External Resources

Certain documents or links redirect to external departmental portals or national government portals. External websites are maintained by their respective organizations, who are responsible for their accessibility.

Feedback & Assistance

If you experience any accessibility difficulties or have suggestions for improving access to any portion of this portal, please contact us at chair-aparc[at]arn[dot]gov[dot]in or visit our Suggestions & Feedback page.
