The Brand Ambassador of APARC.

26 May 2026

Miss Geyin Miyu has extended her support to the Commission by agreeing to serve as the Brand Ambassador of APARC on 25th May 2026.

Miss Geyin Miyu, a well-known actress, content creator, and researcher from Arunachal Pradesh, has established a strong presence through her contributions to culture, entertainment, and youth engagement across the state. Her voluntary association with APARC reflects her commitment towards promoting public awareness, responsible governance, and positive social initiatives. The Commission sincerely appreciates her goodwill gesture and willingness to support the vision and objectives of APARC in strengthening citizen participation and administrative reforms in Arunachal Pradesh. Present during the occasion were: Shri Pramod Jain, IAS (Retd.) – Chairman, APARC Shri Hento Karga, APCS (Retd.) – Member, APARC Shri Biplov Sur,APSS– Deputy Secretary,APARC Ms. Omey Apang,APCS– Under Secretary, APARC APARC expresses its gratitude to Miss Geyin Miyu for associating with the Commission and looks forward to meaningful collaboration in reaching out to the people of the state, especially the youth, with the message of progressive governance and administrative transformation.
