Citizen Voice

Why Your Suggestions Matter

The Administrative Reforms Commission (APARC) believes that lasting administrative transformation is built upon genuine public participation. Whether you are a student, government employee, teacher, entrepreneur, or grassroots community elder, your direct insights help pinpoint bottlenecks, eliminate red tape, and design better public services.

Service Delivery

Suggest ideas to digitize licenses, speed up certificates, and make public offices more friendly and accessible.

Civil Service Reforms

Recommend measures to improve continuous training, meritocracy, transparent postings, and employee motivation.

District Administration

Share suggestions for rural grievance resolution, district office modernization, and devolution of power.
