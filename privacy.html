Privacy Policy

Arunachal Pradesh Administrative Reforms Commission

Home / Policies & Help / Privacy Policy

The Administrative Reforms Commission (APARC) of Arunachal Pradesh does not automatically capture any specific personal information from you (like name, phone number, or e-mail address) that allows us to identify you individually. If the website requests you to provide personal information, you will be informed of the particular purposes for which the information is gathered and adequate security measures will be taken to protect your personal information.

We do not sell or share any personally identifiable information volunteered on this site to any third party (public/private). Any information provided to this website will be protected from loss, misuse, unauthorized access or disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

We gather certain technical information about the User, such as Internet protocol (IP) addresses, domain name, browser type, operating system, the date and time of the visit, and the pages visited. We make no attempt to link these addresses with the identity of individuals visiting our site unless an attempt to damage or breach the site has been detected.

Cookies may be used strictly for accessibility preferences (such as remembering text size adjustments) and do not store any personally identifiable data.
