About the Commission

The Administrative Reforms Commission (ARC) of Arunachal Pradesh has been constituted by the Government of Arunachal Pradesh to examine the current scope and functioning of the State's administrative system and to suggest actionable steps for its continuous improvement in the light of changing socio-economic circumstances.

The Commission is committed to reviewing the existing administrative architecture, enhancing civil service efficiency, optimizing public resource management, and recommending transformative reforms to create a more responsive, inclusive, and modern governance ecosystem.
