Commission Members Submit Preliminary Report

23 Feb 2026

The Arunachal Pradesh Administrative Reforms Commission has submitted its Preliminary Report to the Hon’ble Chief Minister.

This is an important step towards improving governance, strengthening administrative efficiency, and making public service delivery more transparent, accountable, and citizen-friendly.

The Commission is also inviting valuable suggestions, feedback, and recommendations from the public, students, employees, civil society organizations, and all concerned citizens.
