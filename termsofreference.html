Mandate

Scope of the Commission

The Administrative Reforms Commission has been entrusted with reviewing key dimensions of governance across Arunachal Pradesh to deliver an agile, responsive, and citizen-friendly administration. The formal Terms of Reference are structured under five major focus areas:

  • Review the existing departmental structure and recommend streamlining measures to eliminate redundancies and improve inter-departmental coordination.
  • Analyze workload distribution across departments and propose structural realignment wherever required.
  • Evaluate the feasibility of creating new departments or merging existing ones to better address emerging socio-economic challenges.
  • Revisit and redefine the role of departments in the context of the evolving nature of governance and collaborative administration.
  • Conduct manpower planning and workflow process re-engineering.
  • Suggest ways to position administrative services in the modern context of regional development, digital integration, and economic empowerment.
  • Examine if the present administrative machinery is optimally suited to the current developmental needs and the unique socio-cultural context of the State.
  • Recommend a balanced framework identifying areas where regulatory oversight should be strengthened and areas where bureaucratic procedures should be simplified.
  • Strengthen the framework for efficient, economical, sensitive, clean, objective, and agile governance.
  • Review recruitment policies and procedures for state government positions, aiming for utmost transparency and meritocracy.
  • Develop a comprehensive, continuous training and capacity building program including ethics in governance for civil servants at all levels to enhance their skills, professional knowledge, and public connect.
  • Analyze existing staff cadres and recommend an efficient structure considering career progression, motivation, and performance management.
  • Promote gender equality, equity, and inclusion within civil services through review of recruitment and career advancement policies.
  • Review policies relating to recruitment methods, institutional training, and field placement.
  • Provide clear guidelines for enhancing the productivity and appraisal of civil servants.
  • Study various existing cadres and recommend optimal cadre management keeping in mind the State's long-term interests and public service delivery goals.
  • Strategize for upgrading skills and competencies through targeted institutional interventions.
  • Directly link the performance of civil servants to tangible social and economic outcomes.
  • Bring about systemic changes to infuse and sustain operational vibrancy, responsiveness, and accountability at the district and subdivision levels.
  • Streamline and fine-tune a comprehensive, accessible, and prompt public grievance redressal mechanism.
  • Devolve and delegate administrative powers, financial functions, and operational resources to local authorities.
  • Examine the coordinating and leadership role of the District Collector / Deputy Commissioner in developmental activities and community participation.
  • Modernize and transform district administrative infrastructure in form and content, keeping in mind its frontline role in delivering services to grassroots communities.
  • Invest in and promote social capital across all administrative tiers to heighten government effectiveness and public trust.
  • Enhance the administrative capacity to proactively partner with local communities, indigenous institutions, and remote border villages.
  • Strengthen synergy between the government administration and Community-Based Organizations (CBOs) / civil society.
  • Increase the citizen-centricity of administrative approaches and institutional communication.
  • Ensure active involvement of people's representatives and community elders in project conceptualization and implementation monitoring.
  • Develop a unified e-governance roadmap to digitize public services, automate departmental workflows, and expand public information portals.
  • Prioritize robust cybersecurity protocols, data privacy safeguards, and interoperability standards across all official applications.
  • Reduce red tape, delays, and bureaucratic friction through technology interventions, digital dashboards, and AI-assisted governance tools.
  • Promote state-wide knowledge sharing, best-practice databases, and digital skill upgrades across all administrative cadres.
