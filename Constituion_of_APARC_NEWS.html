Constitution of the Arunachal Pradesh Administrative Reforms Commission.

23 Feb 2026

Arunachal Pradesh approved the constitution of the Arunachal Pradesh Administrative Reforms Commission (APARC)

The Government of Arunachal Pradesh approved the constitution of the Arunachal Pradesh Administrative Reforms Commission (APARC) on 28 October 2024 with the objective of reviewing the existing administrative system and recommending reforms for improving governance, efficiency, transparency, and public service delivery in the State. The Commission has been constituted to examine various aspects of administration and suggest measures for making the government machinery more responsive, accountable, citizen-centric, and result-oriented. The initiative reflects the commitment of the State Government towards strengthening administrative processes, improving institutional performance, and ensuring better delivery of services to the people of Arunachal Pradesh. Members of the public, stakeholders, government employees, civil society organizations, and concerned citizens are encouraged to share their valuable suggestions and feedback to support the reform process.
